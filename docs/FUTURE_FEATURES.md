# DayLoop Future Features & Production Polish Roadmap

This document outlines proposed future features and enhancements to elevate DayLoop's capability, intelligence, and consumer polish.

---

## 1. Natural Language Quick Capture (NLP)
- **Concept**: Allow typing natural phrasing into quick-add (e.g., *"Gym every weekday at 7am for 45 mins"*) and have DayLoop automatically parse it into structured recurrence, time blocks, and reminder policies.
- **Goal**: Friction-free, conversational task creation.

## 2. Interactive Glance & Lock Screen Widgets
- **Concept**: Upgrade Android Glance widgets to support **direct task completion and checkbox toggles** directly from home screen and lock screen widgets without opening the main app.
- **Goal**: Minimize app-launch friction for routine execution.

## 3. Focus Heatmaps & Productivity Analytics
- **Concept**: A dedicated Insights / Review tab showing GitHub-style contribution heatmaps for focus minutes, task completion velocity, and project progress distribution over 30 to 365 days.
- **Goal**: Reinforce positive habit loops and long-term planning.

## 4. Smart Morning Briefing
- **Concept**: A calm morning greeting card on Today summarizing workload, scheduling density, and focus recommendations.
- **Goal**: Proactive, respectful productivity guidance.

## 5. Full-App Encrypted Peer-to-Peer Sync (WebDAV / Local Network)
- **Concept**: Extend the encrypted wardrobe transfer capability into an optional, end-to-end encrypted peer-to-peer sync protocol supporting WebDAV or local network syncing across multiple devices without requiring a central cloud backend.
- **Goal**: Maintain 100% user privacy and offline autonomy while enabling multi-device workflows.
